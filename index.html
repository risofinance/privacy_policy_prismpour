Privacy Policy for PrismPour

App Name: PrismPour  |  Package Name: com.aistudio.prismpour.kxqvmr
Effective Date: November 25, 2026  |  Developer Contact: risofinance@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how PrismPour ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application on Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with Google Play Developer Program policies.

1. Information We Collect Directly

PrismPour does not require account registration, sign-in, or personal details such as your name, phone number, email address, or photos. We do not directly collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information on our own servers.

Local On-Device Storage: Your gameplay progress — including unlocked levels, earned stars, virtual coins, daily challenge streaks, unlocked themes, sound/haptic preferences, and colorblind symbol settings — is saved strictly on your own device using Android DataStore.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To keep PrismPour free to play, the App displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

Google AdMob and its certified advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and improve ad relevance. Depending on your region and consent settings, this may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location
  • Device model, operating system version, screen size, and language
  • App interaction and ad impression/click diagnostics

You can review how Google handles data through these official links:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Data in Advertising (AdMob)
  • Google Play Services Terms

3. Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You may control or opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your Android device by opening: Settings → Google → Ads and selecting Delete advertising ID or opting out of ad personalization.

4. App Permissions

PrismPour follows the principle of least privilege and requests only the minimal install-time permissions needed to display ads:

  • INTERNET: Required solely to load advertisements from Google AdMob.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to check network availability before requesting ads.

PrismPour does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs, photos/media storage, or precise GPS location.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all game progress is stored locally on your device, you can permanently delete all App data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → PrismPour → Storage → Clear Storage) or by uninstalling PrismPour. For data collected by Google AdMob, retention and deletion are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

PrismPour is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable digital age of consent in your country, such as 16 in certain EEA member states). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at risofinance@gmail.com so we can promptly address the request.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California), you may have rights regarding data processed by third-party advertising providers, including the right to withdraw consent, opt out of personalized ads, or request information about data processing. You can exercise these controls through your Android Advertising ID settings and Google's privacy tools, or by contacting us directly.

8. Security

All network requests made by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections. Local preferences remain protected within Android's sandboxed application storage.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, Google Play policies, or App features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
